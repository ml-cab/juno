# 9.6. Export Control

> This document is not legal advice. Consult a qualified attorney for specific decisions.

Juno is cryptography-free software. It does not implement or bundle encryption algorithms and
therefore is not subject to EAR or ITAR cryptography controls under US export regulations.

However, LLM technology and GPU compute are subject to evolving US and EU export control rules.
Operators deploying Juno in cross-border or government contexts should review current Commerce
Department (BIS) Entity List and EAR Part 744 restrictions independently. The Juno project makes
no representations about the export status of the software or of third-party model weights
loaded by it.
